Overhand Shot (os)

This is a fundamental shot in WDB which simply involves hopping over the ball and grappling it. The movement will cause the ball to swing over the top of the player. Due to the simplicity of this shot, it can be seen in several other shots. However, in its base form, its strength is average and its swing path very predictable.

Plunging Overhand Shot (Pos)

This shot is defined by the steep angle of the shot that comes with shooting around Zone 3. It works off the premise of Plunge Path Range (ppr) which takes advantage of the fact that the fastest defensive maneuver is upwards via weighted grapple hops. As such, the strength of Pos is that it can shut down a defender�s defensive aerial movement from happening. A weakness that comes with Plunge is that, if the opponent manages a counter, it may force you to play Float Plunge Defense on a shot with considerable momentum.

Overhand Shot Loop (osL)

This shot is defined by how the Player swings the ball around them in circles. The strength of Loop is that it forces the Opponent to be on guard for a longer period of time as opposed to a normal shot. Coupled with that, performing multiple loops allows you to narrow down a shot path that is favorable. As such, the defense has to work overtime to cover each and every shot possibility. If they make a wrong prediction too early, they can be punished by your next loop. The weaknesses of Loop are that it consumes a lot of Grapple Charge, can be disrupted via Jousts, and requires air time. If you get hit by a strong counter after Looping for a while, you may not have enough charge to perform a Goal Extension Recovery.
